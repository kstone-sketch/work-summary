    2020 Needs a Kick in the GlassSummer 2020

    When the pandemic shut down the bars that built Ghost, the brand answered a stressed-out country with a joke instead of silence. The line ran on roughly 300 billboards in Texas, Massachusetts and Rhode Island, ad space that went cheap when everyone else went quiet. Behind the humor was a hard pivot from bars to retail, and it worked: Ghost tripled its sales and turned its worst-case year into its best.
